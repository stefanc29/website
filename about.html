About

I am a musician, producer, and engineer working in experimental and alternative music. I have produced and recorded for bands such as Jane Street and Flaco and fully produce, write, and engineer my own solo project under my full name Stefan Ceara. I am interested in sound design for animation and movies, electroacoustic music, and general audio weirdness.
